Instructions

  • While you are responding to the number memory game or watching the robot's activity, the robot may ask you question if an obstacle prevents it from putting the bag on one of the berths. A dialog box will appear on the screen during this time and you will be asked choose an option.
  • Please be careful, as the robot can sometime have wrong perception about the location of the block on the berths. So, if a robot asks you for help, it is always advised to look at the video to see the location of the block before responding to the robot. For example, if the robot thinks Berth1 is blocked but in the video if a different berth is shown blocked, please respond by choosing option "No" or specific berth --> "Berth1", "Berth2" , "Berth3" from the options to rectify the robot's mistake.
  • It is always preferred to put the cargo bag on the nearest berth. As it will take lesser time for the robot to put the bag on the berth and also reduces the risk of getting stuck in the obstacle. For example, if Berth2 or Berth3 is blocked (in the video) the robot can drop the cargo bag on Berth1. If Berth1 is blocked the robot can put the cargo bag on Berth2.
