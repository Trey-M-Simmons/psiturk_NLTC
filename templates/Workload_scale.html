Instructions

  • During this experiment you will be asked to enter your current workload periodically. Here the term "workload" means your current mental stress level.
  • The workload scale has numbers ranging from 1-3. 1 denotes the lowest level of your current workload, 2 denotes the moderate level of your current workload, and 3 denotes the highest level of your current workload. When you are asked to choose a number please select a number from that range indicating your current mental stress from the experiment. Once you choose the number you will be redirected back to your previous window.
