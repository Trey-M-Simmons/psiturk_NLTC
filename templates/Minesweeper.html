Instructions

  • Minesweeper is a puzzle game where you click on an uncovered square to reveal a blank cell, a number or a "mine". If the uncovered cell reveals a number, it will indicate the number of mines placed diagonally and/or adjacent to it. If the uncovered cell reveals a blank tile, all the adjacent non-mined cells will be automatically uncovered. If the uncovered cell revelas a mine, the game is over. There is only one life per game.
