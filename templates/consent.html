We need your consent to proceed

You have been invited to take part in the research study:

Purpose of Research: This research study is being conducted to study how humans and robots interact with each other in a collaborative task.
What you will do in this study: If you decide on participating in this study, you will be asked to fill out a brief demographic survey, test your audio and video quality and play a number memory game and help a simulated robot followed by a set of instructions.
Time required: The session will take approximately 15 minutes to complete.
Risks and discomfort: There are no major foreseeable risks with this study.
Benefits: There are no direct personal benefits.
Compensation: You will receive $3.00 for completion at the end of the study.
Confidentiality: Your data and consent form will be kept separate. Your research data will be stored in a secure location on Mines property. Sharing of data with other researchers will only be done in such a manner that you will not be identified. By participating, you understand and agree that the data and information gathered during this study may be used by Mines and published and/or disclosed by Mines to others outside of Mines. However, your name, contact information, and other direct personal identifiers will not be mentioned in any such publication or dissemination of the research data and/or results by Mines. Note that per regulation all research data must be kept for a minimum of 3 years.
Participation and Withdrawal: Your participation is completely voluntary. Should you decide at any time you no longer wish to participate you may discontinue your participation without penalty.
To contact the researcher: If you have questions or concerns about this research please contact the principal investigator: Tom Williams, MIRROR Lab, Colorado School of Mines, 1600 Illinois St. Golden, CO, twilliams@mines.edu.
Whom to contact about your rights in this research: For questions, concerns, suggestions, or complaints that are not being addressed by the researcher, or research ­related harm, contact the Colorado School of Mines Human-Subject Research Office at humansubjects@mines.edu.
By accepting this HIT, you confirm that you understand the above material, that you are willing to participate in this experiment, and that you allow us to use your data for scientific purposes, and may be published or otherwise shared with other researchers. No identifying information will ever be shared.

Do you understand and consent to these terms?
